About

Athen is a civic engagement app. Enter your ZIP. Get a feed of the bills, votes, and elections that affect you — federal, state, and local — explained in plain English by Claude AI. One tap to write your representative, sign a petition, or get a personalized plan for a life event.

Built by Chris DiMarco. Non-partisan. No ads. No data sold. Available on the App Store.

What's inside

  • AI Brief — every bill explained with key facts, both sides, fiscal impact, and what happens if you do nothing
  • Voter Hub — next election countdown, sample ballot, polling place, registration deadlines, rep scorecard
  • Petitions — sign or start a petition; Athen handles delivery
  • Officials Directory — every elected official in the US, searchable
  • Life Events — describe what's happening (job loss, eviction, new baby) and get a structured civic plan
  • Read Aloud — ElevenLabs voice reads any brief, plan, or profile

Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-05-19 · Contact: chris@logicoutloud.com

What we collect

As little as possible. Most of what you do stays on your phone.

Stays on your device only

  • Your ZIP code (used to find your representatives)
  • Your civic context tags (renter, parent, business owner)
  • Your topic interests (housing, transit, etc.)
  • Your filing receipts and bookmarks
  • Your social posts and comments inside the Square tab
  • App preferences

This data is stored in iOS UserDefaults and never leaves the device unless you explicitly trigger an action.

Sent to the Athen backend when you trigger an action

  • Endorse / Oppose: the issue identifier, your ZIP, and the message you reviewed. Backend resolves your rep and drafts text via Anthropic Claude. The draft is returned to your phone and saved to your local filing history. In v1, Athen does not send the message to the official — direct delivery rolls out in a later release. If you opt in to Composio Gmail, Athen sends from your own address with your confirmation.
  • Assistant chat: your message and a redacted snapshot of your visible feed go to Claude via our backend. Not retained after the response.
  • Briefs: backend reads its own issue cache and may call Claude. No personal data sent — briefs are written once per bill and shared.
  • Audio briefing: Claude writes script, ElevenLabs renders speech. Only the script text goes to ElevenLabs.
  • Square Report: we record the post ID, your reported reason, and your per-install UUID for moderation review.

Third-party services

  • Anthropic Claude — drafting, briefs, plans, chat
  • ElevenLabs — text-to-speech
  • Composio — opt-in OAuth bridge for Gmail/Calendar/Notion/Slack/etc.
  • OpenStates, Congress.gov, GovInfo, OpenSecrets — read-only public data
  • Google Civic Information API — polling places + ballots (ZIP only)
  • Firecrawl — read-only news + government site scraping

What we don't collect

  • No name, email, or phone (no account required)
  • No precise location (ZIP only)
  • No contacts, photos beyond what you attach, biometrics
  • No advertising IDs, no cross-app tracking, no data brokers
  • No browsing or search history

Your rights

  • Delete the app to remove all local data
  • Revoke any Composio OAuth in your Composio account
  • Email chris@logicoutloud.com to delete petition signatures stored on backend

Terms of Service

Effective: 2026-05-19

1. Who can use Athen

Anyone in the United States with an iPhone and a ZIP code. We don't gate by age but the app is rated 12+ due to political content.

2. What Athen is and isn't

Athen is informational. We surface government data and explain it. We do not provide legal, electoral, financial, or medical advice. We are not affiliated with any government body or political party.

3. AI-generated content

Briefs, drafted letters, and life-event plans are generated by Claude AI and presented for your review. They may contain errors. Always verify before taking action you can't reverse.

4. Filings (constituent letters)

In this release, every filing is recorded locally with a confirmation code. Direct delivery to officials' offices is rolling out in a later release. If you connect Gmail via Composio, sends go from your own address with your explicit confirmation.

5. Petitions

You may start or sign petitions. We dispatch delivered petitions to the target official's office on the petition deadline or threshold. By signing, you assert your identity is your own.

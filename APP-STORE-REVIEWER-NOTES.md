# Athen — App Store Review Notes

Hello reviewer — thank you for your time. Below is everything you need to evaluate Athen end-to-end. Submission contact: chris@logicoutloud.com.

## What Athen is

Athen is a non-partisan civic engagement app. It pulls bills, votes, elections, and government data from official sources (Congress.gov, OpenStates, Federal Register, GovInfo, Google Civic, OpenSecrets), explains it in plain English using AI, and gives users one-tap tools to contact their elected representatives, sign petitions, and get personalized civic plans for life events (job loss, eviction, new baby, etc.).

The app is informational. No financial transactions. No medical advice. No third-party login. No tracking.

## How to test (no credentials needed)

1. Launch the app on a fresh install.
2. When prompted, enter ZIP code **19103** (Philadelphia) — this is a well-populated jurisdiction with federal + state + local data already seeded.
3. (Optional) Skip the interests step or pick "Housing" + "Climate" — both have live items.
4. Land on Home. You should see:
   - A time-of-day greeting card
   - An AI Brief of the day card (tap "Listen" to test ElevenLabs TTS)
   - Tiles for Voter Hub, Officials Directory, Petitions, Life Event front door
5. Tap any item in the feed → opens Brief detail with translate, explain-for, and read-aloud.
6. Tap "Endorse" or "Oppose" on a bill → drafts a letter (~5 sec, AI-generated) → review → Send. **The receipt clearly says "Recorded in your history" — Athen does not send actual emails to officials in this release** (see "Filings" section below).
7. Open Profile → Connections to see the third-party integration list (all opt-in via Composio OAuth).
8. Open Profile → Blocked accounts to test the moderation flow.
9. Long-press any post in Square to test the Report + Block UI.

## Filings (constituent letters) — important for review

Tapping "Endorse" or "Oppose" on a bill generates an AI-drafted letter and saves it to the user's local filing history with channel `recordedLocal`. The receipt and UI explicitly state: *"Recorded in your history. Direct delivery to officials is rolling out in a later release."*

The app does **not** send actual emails to government officials in this release. Direct delivery via verified sending domain (Postmark) is queued for v1.1. Users who connect their own Gmail account via Composio (opt-in) can send from their own address, but this requires their explicit OAuth grant.

We chose to ship this way to avoid:
- Spamming officials' inboxes during early adoption
- Deliverability issues that would burn our sending domain reputation
- Misleading users into thinking their message was delivered when it might be silently filtered

## User-generated content (Square)

Athen's Square is a public feed where users can post about civic actions. Compliance with Guideline 1.2:

- **EULA**: In-app Terms of Service includes a "User-generated content & community standards" section (Section 13). Users acknowledge before posting.
- **Report mechanism**: Long-press any post → "Report post" → categorized reasons (spam, harassment, threats, election misinformation, self-harm, impersonation, other). Reports hide the post locally and queue for review.
- **Block mechanism**: Long-press any post → "Block @user". Blocked accounts' posts and comments disappear from the user's feed. Unblock at any time via Settings → Blocked accounts.
- **Review SLA**: 24 hours for reported content. Posts/comments verified as violating standards are removed and authors blocked.
- **Athen does not allow** content depicting minors in sexual contexts, threats of violence, hate speech targeting protected classes, election misinformation, self-harm promotion, or impersonation. See Section 13 of Terms in-app.

## AI-generated content disclosure

Every AI brief in the app is labeled "ATHEN BRIEF" with a confidence chip. Letters drafted with AI are presented for user review before sending. Plans generated for life events show the source ("generated by Athen, powered by Claude").

The AI is used for:
- Summarizing publicly available bills and rules (Claude)
- Drafting constituent letters from the user's stated stance (Claude)
- Generating civic action plans from user-described life events (Claude)
- Reading the above aloud (ElevenLabs Speech Engine)

Athen does not use AI for medical, legal, or financial advice. The Life Events feature explicitly refers users to government programs (with links) and never to private financial products.

## Election-related content

Athen indexes publicly available government data (bills, votes, elections, candidates). The app is non-partisan:
- Sample-ballot briefs present neutral YES/NO framing with arguments from both sides
- The Rep Scorecard shows how representatives voted on bills the user filed about — pure data, no editorial spin
- Athen does not advocate for parties, candidates, or specific votes
- Athen does not register voters (it links users to their state's official portal)

## Data we collect

See `PRIVACY-NUTRITION-LABELS.md` in this submission package for the exact App Store Connect privacy questionnaire answers. TL;DR:

- **ZIP code** (used to look up your reps; not linked to your identity)
- **Filing history** (stored locally; sent to backend only when you opt-in to Gmail delivery)
- **Civic interests + factors** (e.g. "homeowner", "parent" — stored locally, sent to backend only when generating personalized briefs)
- **Per-install UUID** (anonymous, for rate-limiting; not linked to any other identifier)

We do not collect: name, email, phone, location (beyond ZIP), contacts, photos beyond what users choose to attach to posts, browsing history, advertising identifiers, biometric data, health data, financial data.
